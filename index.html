How to read this dashboard

  • KPIs and targets are taken from Grant Agreement GAP-101239638, Part B (Objectives 1-4, section 1.2.3.1 Riga & Vilnius Mega Pilot, Table 2.1.2-a "KPIs Baseline and Targets"). The GA notes that baselines and definitions are provisional and will be finalised in T5.1 / D5.1.
  • Baseline. By default the baseline is the last value observed before the project start (1 January 2026), i.e. the latest year ≤ 2025. For monthly series the baseline is the mean of the baseline year and "latest" is the mean of the last 12 available months. The shaded band marks the project period (Jan 2026 - Jun 2029, 42 months); the x-axis runs to 2029 so future observations can be compared directly against the pre-project trend.
  • Δ vs baseline is coloured by the desired direction of the KPI (green ▼/▲ = moving towards the GA target, red = away). It is a descriptive change, not an impact estimate: impact attribution needs pilot-area data and a counterfactual (e.g. difference-in-differences against comparable municipalities, or the national trend shown here).
  • GA target line (optional) draws baseline × (1 + target %) at the end of the project. For national or regional proxies it is shown only as a scale reference; the GA targets refer to the pilot target areas.
  • Geographic level is printed beside every series (Municipality, City, NUTS 2 region, National, Monitoring station, EU benchmark). The Vilnius pilot is in Vilnius District Municipality (Vilniaus r. sav.), three PUMH mobility hubs; Riga pilot is led by the Riga Planning Region.

Known data gaps

  • KPI 5, 7, 8, 10, 11 and 17 have no public data - they rely on pilot / partner records (participation registers, Citizen-Voice and VR/AR logs, logistics operator data, municipal permit registers). KPI 8 shows national freight context only.
  • KPI 9 (congestion): no public congestion series for Vilnius District; the traffic-counter dataset (data.gov.lt/datasets/1249) blocks cloud downloads and must be retrieved locally; Riga needs city traffic data / TomTom index.
  • Air quality: no EEA station inside the Vilnius District pilot area (city stations shown); Riga station network changed - no valid annual values 2022-2024.
  • Lithuanian vehicle register series have a methodology break between 2013 and 2014; series start in 2014.
  • 2026 air-quality values are year-to-date (partial year).

Updating

Re-run the download notebook (BIO_INTEL_MOB_KPI_duomenu_atsisiuntimas.ipynb), then python3 build_dashboard.py in the KPI_dashboard folder. It rebuilds the embedded data and this HTML file. Pilot-collected KPIs can be added as new series in build_dashboard.py.
